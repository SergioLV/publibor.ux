# Publibor Brand Identity

## Company
Publibor is a Chilean textile production company based in Santiago (Ureta Cox 233, San Joaquín). 20+ years in operation. B2B supplier — not retail.

## Core services
- DTF (direct-to-film printing)
- Bordado (embroidery)
- Corte láser (laser cutting for insignias)
- Producción recurrente (recurring production runs)

## Who they serve
- Marcas (brands that outsource textile production)
- Empresas (companies needing uniforms, merch, promotional items)
- Distribuidores (distributors and resellers)
- Instituciones (government, schools, organizations via ChileCompra)

## Brand colors
- Black: `#000000` / Near-black: `#0c0c0c`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Publibor Gold (accent only): `#F3C242`
- Yellow must never dominate. Black and white lead. Gold sharpens.

## Brand font
- Chakra Petch (Google Fonts) for the brand wordmark only
- System UI stack for all body text

## Logo
- File: `/pb_logo.svg` — gear-shaped badge with "PUBLIBOR CHILE" text baked in
- White version via CSS `filter: brightness(0) invert(1)` on dark backgrounds
- Used as favicon and header/footer mark
- No separate wordmark needed — the logo contains the name

## Brand tone
- Direct, commercial, operationally grounded
- Sounds like a real supplier, not a strategist or agency
- Uses industry language: pedidos, reposición, corridas, cotización, cumplimiento, terminaciones
- Avoids: "partner estratégico", "solución integral", "innovación", "escalabilidad", startup/SaaS language
