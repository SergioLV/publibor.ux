# Publibor Copy Rules

## Language
- All copy in Spanish (Chilean market)
- Direct, commercial, operationally grounded
- Sounds like a supplier talking to another business, not a brand deck

## Tone
- Firm, confident, concise
- Commercially aware — understands deadlines, repeat orders, client pressure
- Human but not casual — professional without being stiff

## Preferred vocabulary
- producción para terceros
- bordado, DTF, corte láser
- pedidos recurrentes, reposición
- cotización, requerimiento
- cumplimiento, consistencia entre corridas
- terminaciones, desarrollo de pedidos
- marcas, empresas, distribuidores, instituciones
- deporte, merchandising, uniformes, campañas

## Vocabulary to avoid
- partner estratégico / socio de producción (overused)
- B2B (don't label it — show it)
- externalizar con confianza
- continuidad operativa
- solución integral / alto impacto
- innovación textil / excelencia operacional
- escalabilidad, workflows, growth
- any English buzzwords

## Sentence rules
- Short to medium sentences
- One idea per sentence
- Concrete nouns, commercial verbs
- Max 2-3 lines per paragraph
- If a line sounds like a consultant wrote it, rewrite it

## Section copy limits
- Eyebrows: 2-4 words
- Headlines: max 2 lines
- Supporting text: max 2-3 lines
- Service descriptions: 1-2 sentences
- Form labels: 1 word

## Copy test
Before accepting any line, ask:
1. Does this sound like someone from the industry would say it?
2. Does this sound like a supplier, not a strategist?
3. Can this be said in fewer words?
4. Is this repeating an earlier idea?
