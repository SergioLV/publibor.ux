# Publibor Landing Direction

## Objective

Design and build the Publibor landing page in Astro with a level of quality, restraint, and commercial clarity closer to **MFG Merch**, but adapted to Publibor’s reality, market, and visual identity.

The final result should feel like a **serious textile production company** that works with brands, companies, resellers, merchandising suppliers, sports-related clients, and institutional requirements.

It must feel:
- premium
- grounded
- commercially credible
- visually intentional
- modern without looking trendy
- restrained without feeling bland

It must **not** feel:
- like a startup SaaS landing page
- like a generic AI-generated template
- like a retail custom t-shirt shop
- like a wall of text
- like a Tailwind component dump
- like a “clean but empty” design

---

## Main reference

Use **MFG Merch** as the main reference for:
- overall seriousness
- premium B2B/commercial posture
- restraint
- strong image-led sections
- spacing and confidence
- polished but non-gimmicky execution

Do **not** copy their website directly.

Instead, take inspiration from:
- the confidence of the layout
- the controlled use of imagery
- the premium commercial feel
- the sense that the business serves real companies and repeat accounts

Publibor should feel **closer to that level of maturity**, but with its own identity:
- more tied to textile production
- more grounded in DTF, bordado, texturizado, and producción para terceros
- less corporate-merch agency, more production supplier

---

## Brand foundation

### Colors
Use only these as the core palette:

- Black: `#000000`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Publibor Yellow: `#F3C242`

Yellow must be used as an **accent**, not as the dominant color of the page.

The page should be mostly driven by:
- black
- white
- subtle neutrals
- controlled yellow emphasis

Do not introduce additional accent colors.

---

## Positioning

Publibor is not a retail custom apparel store.

Publibor should be positioned as:
- a serious production supplier
- a provider for third-party production
- a company that supports recurring orders
- a partner for brands, companies, resellers, merchandising suppliers, and institutional buyers
- a supplier that understands continuity, deadlines, replenishment, and commercial execution

This must be visible in both:
- the design
- the copy tone
- the image selection
- the section structure

---

## Tone of the page

The page should speak in the language of the industry, not generic “B2B brand language”.

Avoid overly abstract wording like:
- strategic partner
- scalable solutions
- operational excellence
- empowering brands
- trusted B2B textile solutions
- growth-oriented production

Prefer language closer to:
- producción para terceros
- bordado y DTF
- pedidos recurrentes
- reposición
- proveedor confiable
- respuesta comercial
- cumplimiento
- trabajo para marcas, empresas y revendedores
- requerimientos promocionales, deportivos e institucionales
- terminaciones y desarrollo de pedidos

The tone should feel:
- direct
- commercially real
- serious
- confident
- concise
- grounded

---

## Main design correction

The current direction is too text-heavy and too flat.

The new version must solve:
- blandness
- too much explanation
- repetitive section structure
- lack of visual authority
- lack of strong image direction

The fix is **not** to add trendy UI.

The fix is:
- reduce copy
- improve hierarchy
- create stronger section contrast
- use imagery with more authority
- make the composition feel more designed

---

## Core design principles

### 1. Fewer words, more conviction
Every section must say less and communicate more.

Avoid long paragraphs.  
Use:
- shorter copy blocks
- stronger headlines
- sharper supporting lines
- cleaner scan paths

The page should feel easier to read in 10 seconds.

---

### 2. Composition before components
Do not solve the page with cards, icons, or repeated UI patterns.

Use:
- strong section composition
- asymmetry where appropriate
- image-text balance
- typography hierarchy
- spacing and contrast

Do not make the page feel assembled from components.

---

### 3. Images must do real work
Images should not just decorate the page.  
They must reinforce:
- production
- materiality
- seriousness
- process
- repetition
- result

The website should feel tactile and commercial.

Avoid:
- generic office people
- generic smiling models
- fake startup graphics
- floating mockups
- lifestyle-first imagery
- AI-looking image compositions

Prefer:
- close-up textile detail
- bordado detail
- DTF texture or application
- controlled production environment
- grouped products / repeated units
- folded garments / organized batches
- detail shots that feel premium and real
- one strong dark hero visual rather than many weak visuals

---

### 4. Typography carries the brand
Typography should do a large part of the visual work.

Use:
- strong headings
- disciplined line lengths
- consistent spacing
- visible hierarchy between label, title, body, and CTA

Avoid:
- giant paragraphs
- oversized text everywhere
- decorative font gimmicks
- too many weight changes

The page should feel premium because it is well designed, not because it is flashy.

---

### 5. Visual rhythm matters
The page should not feel like:
headline + paragraph + section  
headline + paragraph + section  
headline + paragraph + section

Create rhythm using:
- dark and light section contrast
- image-led vs text-led sections
- compressed sections vs breathing sections
- varied layout proportions
- stronger transitions in pacing

The page should feel directed, not mechanical.

---

## Copy direction

The copy should sound like a real supplier in this category.

Do not overuse the term:
- B2B
- partner
- strategic
- externalize with trust
- operational continuity
- scalable solutions

Prefer simpler, more industry-native phrasing.

Examples of the right direction:
- Producción textil para marcas, empresas y revendedores
- Bordado, DTF y producción para terceros
- Un proveedor serio para pedidos recurrentes
- Producción para campañas, deporte, promociones y empresas
- Respuesta clara para requerimientos textiles comerciales
- Reposición, continuidad y buena ejecución

Do not let the copy sound like a consultant wrote it.

---

## Recommended page structure

Keep the page relatively tight and intentional.

### 1. Hero
The hero should establish authority fast.

Must include:
- strong eyebrow
- bold but grounded headline
- short supporting text
- 1–2 clear CTAs
- one strong visual area

The hero should feel premium, controlled, and image-aware.

Possible headline directions:
- Producción textil para marcas, empresas y revendedores.
- Bordado, DTF y producción para terceros.
- Un proveedor serio para pedidos textiles recurrentes.

The hero image should be:
- dark
- tactile
- premium
- close-up
- real
- not busy

Think:
fabric detail, embroidered garment detail, production texture, controlled apparel close-up.

---

### 2. Positioning section
This section should explain Publibor in a few seconds.

It should make clear:
- this is not a consumer-first shop
- Publibor works with commercial requirements
- Publibor supports recurring work, brands, resellers, companies, and institutional needs

Keep it short:
- one strong headline
- one short paragraph
- one highlighted line if useful

No long essay.

---

### 3. Services / capabilities
Show the core capabilities clearly:

- DTF
- Bordado
- Texturizado
- Producción recurrente

This section should be highly scannable.

Avoid generic feature-card design.  
Instead prefer:
- strong rows
- image + text pairings
- compact capability blocks
- editorial list style
- a more premium production-company feel

Each service should have:
- title
- one short line
- maybe an image if it adds authority

---

### 4. Sectors / who it is for
Show the kinds of clients or work contexts Publibor supports.

Examples:
- marcas
- revendedores
- merchandising
- deporte
- empresas
- institucional

This should not become a bloated industry list.

Keep it compact and sharp.  
The message should be:
“we understand the kinds of accounts and orders this market requires.”

---

### 5. Process
A short operational section.

Use 3 or 4 steps max:
- requerimiento
- cotización
- producción
- reposición / continuidad

Short, commercial, direct.

The goal is confidence, not storytelling.

---

### 6. Credibility / why Publibor
Short, high-signal section.

Use themes like:
- respuesta comercial clara
- cumplimiento
- continuidad de trabajo
- producción para terceros
- criterio para pedidos recurrentes
- buena ejecución en bordado, DTF y terminaciones

Avoid long benefits copy.

This section should feel compact and strong.

---

### 7. CTA
The final CTA should feel firm, serious, and commercially useful.

Good directions:
- Solicitar cotización
- Hablemos de tu requerimiento
- Cuéntanos qué necesitas producir
- Revisemos tu próximo pedido

Do not sound needy or overly promotional.

---

## Image direction by section

### Hero image
One strong image only.
Use:
- dark textile close-up
- embroidery detail
- premium material crop
- apparel surface detail
- production-context detail

No collage.

---

### Services images
Use either:
- one image per capability
or
- one shared art direction across all service blocks

Preferred image topics:
- DTF application or transfer detail
- bordado close-up
- texturizado close-up
- stacked/repeated garments for recurrent production

Keep the image treatment consistent.

---

### Client/sector section
Images optional.
This section can be mostly typographic if well designed.

---

### Process section
Images optional.
If used, keep them subtle and operational.

---

### CTA section
Can be text-only or include one dark supporting production image.

Do not overcomplicate it.

---

## UI rules

### Buttons
Buttons should feel solid and controlled.

Primary:
- yellow background
- black text
- rectangular or lightly rounded
- no pill shapes
- no gimmicks

Secondary:
- outlined or black/white
- strong border
- equally intentional

---

### Cards
Use cards only if clearly justified.

Do not default to:
- 4 feature cards
- icon cards
- floating marketing boxes
- equal repeated blocks

If a list or row works better, use that instead.

---

### Icons
Default: do not use icons unless truly necessary.

Typography, spacing, and image direction should do most of the work.

---

### Backgrounds
Use background shifts with purpose.

Recommended:
- black hero
- light content section
- occasional dark emphasis section
- restrained contrast throughout

Avoid too many color changes.

---

### Motion
Minimal only.

Allowed:
- subtle reveal
- button hover
- image hover refinement
- calm transitions

Avoid:
- flashy animation
- floating movement
- excessive parallax
- trendy motion effects

---

## Content density rules

Reduce text density significantly compared to the current version.

Hard direction:
- cut explanatory copy by roughly 30–45%
- let imagery and hierarchy do more work
- do not repeat the same positioning idea in multiple sections

Every section should answer:
- what is this
- why does it matter
- what can I do next

Nothing more unless justified.
